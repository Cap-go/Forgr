Delete your Forgr data

Forgr has no account. Most data never leaves your phone, and you can remove all of it yourself.

On your phone

  • One tool: touch and hold it on the home screen, then tap Delete. The tool, its chat and its files are removed.
  • Everything: uninstall Forgr. Tools, chats, files, saved keys and your ChatGPT sign in are all removed from the phone.
  • ChatGPT sign in: open your profile in Forgr and tap Sign Out.

Forgr Cloud

If your tools used cloud data, server functions, cloud keys or shared spaces, email tori@capgo.app from any address with the subject "Delete my Forgr Cloud data" and the email of the ChatGPT account you sign in with. We delete everything stored for that account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Diagnostics

Forgr no longer sends error reports. Reports from older versions contained no tool content or keys and are deleted automatically after 30 days.
