FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I publish or sell an app I made?

No, and that is on purpose. Mapp is for tiny tools you build for yourself and for interactive explainers that help you learn something by playing with it. Your apps live inside Mapp on your phone; they are not separate products for the App Store.

Do I need to pay?

Mapp itself is free. You need a ChatGPT account, and building uses your plan's normal limits. There is no extra subscription.

Where are my apps stored?

Only on your phone. Your apps and their data are not uploaded to a Mapp server, and there is no Mapp account.

Can I share apps with friends?

Not yet. It is one of the most requested features and it is on our list.

Is there an Android version?

It is coming. Mapp is built with Capacitor, so Android is the natural next step.

Is Mapp affiliated with OpenAI?

No. Mapp is made by Capgo and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI. You simply sign in with your own ChatGPT account.
